## About me

My work is mostly shaped by real systems rather than ideal ones — legacy tools, incomplete documentation, fragmented processes, and teams that still need clear outputs despite all of that.

Over time, that led me toward bottleneck analysis, process mapping, data structuring, and building clearer links between operations and technical requirements.

I work mainly with SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI, and documentation-driven analysis. I'm particularly interested in roles where structure, clarity, and useful reporting matter more than presentation for its own sake.
